The Power (97:1-5) 

97:1


Indeed, ˹it is˺ We ˹Who˺ sent this ˹Quran˺ down on the Night of Glory.[1]

[1] It is also believed that the angels descend with all decreed matters for the whole year during that night (al-qadr also means ordainment or destiny).


97:2


And what will make you realize what the Night of Glory is?



97:3


The Night of Glory is better than a thousand months.



97:4


That night the angels and the ˹holy˺ spirit[1] descend, by the permission of their Lord, for every ˹decreed˺ matter.

[1] i.e., the angel Gabriel.


97:5


It is all peace until the break of dawn.
